# Authority and continuation

Before leaving Explore, read back the concept, scope, constraints and endpoint;
obtain explicit owner confirmation. Before leaving Prepare, present the mapped
and planned work, expected changes, proof and limits; obtain explicit owner
approval covering execution. Neither judgment may be inferred. One response may
provide both. Confirmation alone permits only its named non-execution endpoint,
such as recording or preparing. Approval without confirmation does not leave
Explore. Capture the actor, source, meaning and scope with Jot; capture
preserves existing permission and grants none.

Every entry route inherits these boundaries, including direct execution,
activation, claiming, role delegation, Genesis, Adoption, updates, save, handoff
and resume. A request only to Map or Plan ends with prepared work. Active state,
a passing test, a handoff, invocation, urgency or prior implementation supplies
neither judgment. Reuse existing evidence within its actual scope without
another ceremony. Missing or ambiguous evidence stops only the dependent
transition; diagnostics and authority recovery remain available.

An intent correction returns affected work to Explore. Execution beyond the
approved scope needs renewed approval; ordinary fixes within scope retain it.
Approve inside Judge (Human Reviewed Work, the owner's Human QA), the owner's
Ship it and main delivery retain their own existing gates. Execution approval
does not substitute for them.

## Continue without losing authority

Read the current owner source and corrections, then the assigned parent and
Task. A handoff names both judgments, actor, scope, source references, verified
state and next authorized action. A summary never replaces its sources. Keep
one durable writer; a delegate inherits only the caller's authorized scope.
Never relabel a legacy active record as approved when evidence is absent.

The Spec records both judgments as `**Confirmation:**` and
`**Execution approval:**` links, `[JOT-ID](Wiki page)`, to confirmed owner
intent entries that name the Spec (or its Former ID); one entry may supply both. `convert-tasks
--activate` and `claim` refuse before any write while either is missing,
ambiguous, unconfirmed, superseded by a correction or naming another Spec;
`show --json` reports the same `authority` reading for resume. Landmark-direct
claims are outside this check. These seams validate recorded scope and source
consistency. They cannot authenticate a speaker, judge arbitrary English
permission or stop all direct file writes. Agents must compare the source's actual meaning with
the proposed action. Installed behavioral proof remains separate from source
wording and deterministic tests.
