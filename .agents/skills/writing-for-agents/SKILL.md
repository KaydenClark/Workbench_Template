# Writing for agents

Write the smallest artifact that preserves required meaning. Identify its purpose, audience, owner and [applicable budget](../../../workbench/artifact-budgets.json) first.

Lead with essentials. Use concrete language, consistent terms and one job per paragraph. Keep constraints, exceptions and distinctions explicit; Brevity must not require guessing.

Put facts and rules in their proper owners. Link needed detail with useful context; avoid duplication and link chains. Remove repetition, filler, obvious advice and irrelevant examples. Prefer precise wording to cryptic shorthand. Do not fill the budget.

Read as the intended reader: can they understand it and review it? Verify budget, links and source fidelity. Prune again wherever meaning survives unchanged.
